AI 前沿扩张与地缘政经动荡并存 / AI Frontiers Expand Amidst Rising Geopolitical Turbulence

全球科技界正全速推进 AI 代理化与算力基建，OpenAI 估值激增与 Google 新模型的发布显示出行业对智能体（Agent）生产力的极致追求。金融领域中，地缘战争导致的供应链紧张与通胀担忧加剧，美债收益率攀升及各国出口限制风险成为资本市场关注的焦点。政治层面上，伊朗战事牵动全球外交及国防产业链，能源与安全问题正重塑跨国合作的底色。整体而言，科技的进步与实体经济的压力正在进行一场深度的博弈。

The global tech sector is pushing hard for AI agentization and infrastructure, with OpenAI’s surging valuation and Google’s latest models underscoring the relentless pursuit of Agentic productivity. In finance, supply chain strains and inflation fears driven by the Iran conflict are intensifying, as rising bond yields and potential export controls dominate market concerns. Politically, the war in Iran is reshaping global diplomacy and defense supply chains, forcing a re-evaluation of energy and security priorities. Overall, technological progress is currently locked in a deep tug-of-war with real-world economic pressures.
