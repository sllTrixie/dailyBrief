Today's global focus centers on the volatility of the AI capital market and the complexity of geopolitics. In tech, the cannibalization of search advertising revenue by AI models is becoming apparent, dragging down tech stocks as investors question the ROI of massive AI expenditures. Financially, fears of an 'AI bubble' and earnings pressure have sparked a selloff in the tech sector, while a $6.3bn crypto hack linked to North Korea and resource-related geopolitical risks increase uncertainty. Politically, the approaching US midterm elections are intensifying partisan splits, while the end of the Kherson ceasefire marks an escalation in the Russia-Ukraine conflict. Combined with social unrest in Europe and the Middle East, the world is entering a fragile period of instability.
