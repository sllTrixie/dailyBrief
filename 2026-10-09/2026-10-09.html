Financially, the US corporate debt market is undergoing a painful repricing of AI-related risks, while capital shifts from real estate into equity markets reflect growing risk aversion. Politically, the escalation of the Russia-Ukraine conflict and fluctuating US-Iran tensions remain central, alongside tightening regulations on employment non-compete clauses globally. Overall, the market is pivoting from AI optimism to a rigorous scrutiny of commercial viability and financial returns, as the global economy searches for a new equilibrium.
